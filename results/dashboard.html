The rules

Checked once a week on Friday's close, traded the next Monday. Eight ETFs: SPY (US stocks), EFA (international stocks), EEM (emerging markets), TLT (long bonds), IEF (mid bonds), GLD (gold), DBC (commodities), VNQ (real estate).

  1. Buy only when all five checks pass. At most one new buy a week: the ETF with the biggest 12-month gain.
  2. Hold up to 3 ETFs, each a third of the account.
  3. Sell when the weekly close drops below the 40-week average.

Weeks with no trade are normal. Judge the strategy over months, on STRATEGY trades only.
